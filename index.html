How it works

Three steps, run quietly every day.

01

You log four numbers

Sleep hours, stress level, screen time, and steps — entered once, each evening.

02

The model reads the pattern

A decision tree trained on your history checks today's numbers against the patterns that came before your past dips.

03

You get one clear nudge

If a dip looks likely in the next 48 hours, NeuroNudge suggests one small, specific thing to do about it.

UNDER THE HOOD

A model you could sketch on paper.

NeuroNudge runs on a decision tree, not a black box. Each split is a plain question about your last four inputs, so the path from "here's your data" to "here's why" stays visible the whole way.

  • Trained on your own logged history, not a generic population
  • Re-checked every time you log a new day
  • Every forecast traces back to the exact inputs that produced it
Private by design
